# __Mechanics__

* **Goof** (Buff): No longer grants 3 Energy and draws 2 cards when hitting 10 Goof, but instead adds a Cavort into your Hand. - **Cavort**: Play a copy of your last played Attack or Skill. Exhaust.
* **Misfortune** (Debuff) and **Unfortunate** (Keyword): Instead of Misfortune boosting the damage of Unfortunate Attacks, all enemies take damage equal to their Misfortune when an Unfortunate card is played.
* (RENAMED) **Entomb** (Keyword): Formerly Bury (keyword). While it was theoretically fine to have both Bury (mechanic, move another card) and Bury (keyword, move itself) to match Exhaust's double named behavior, it was way too confusing for one of my friends when a fair amount of the card pool adds Bury (keyword) to cards.

* (NEW) **Grave** (Keyword): Start each combat with this card in your **Sepulchre Pile**.
* (NEW) **Half-Summon**: Summons Osty with some HP missing. Half-Summon 5+2 would result in Osty's current HP going up by 5 and his Max HP going up an additional 2 past that (without being healed), for a total of 7 more Max HP.
* (NEW) **Patty Cake** (Buff): Your next Attack deals additional damage and gives Osty 1 less of this. For example, with 4 Patty Cake, your next Attack deals 4 additional damage and gives Osty 3 Patty Cake. Then Osty's next Attack deals 3 additional damage and gives you 2 Patty Cake.
